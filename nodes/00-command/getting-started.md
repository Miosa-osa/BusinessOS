# Getting Started with BusinessOS

Welcome to your business operating system. Here's how to get started:

## 1. Fill in Your Profile
Go to `nodes/01-user/context.md` and fill in your details.

## 2. Set Up Your Business
Go to `nodes/02-business/context.md` and describe your business.

## 3. Add Your Team
Go to `nodes/05-team/context.md` and list your team members.

## 4. Start Tracking Revenue
Go to `nodes/06-revenue/context.md` and add your revenue streams.

## 5. Use the Daily Rhythm
Every morning, run the boot protocol. Every evening, run shutdown.

## 6. Ingest Signals
After calls, meetings, or decisions — capture the information.
The engine will classify it, route it to the right node, and index it.

## 7. Search Your Knowledge
Use the search to find anything across your entire knowledge base.

## 8. Review Weekly
Use the Command Center to track priorities and blockers.

## Tips
- **Think in nodes** — every area of your business is a node
- **Capture everything** — the engine gets smarter as you add more
- **Review weekly** — the system works best with regular maintenance
- **Use the terminal** — Claude Code can build custom modules for you
